INTRODUCTORY NOTE · FARM LIFE

Welcome to the Farm Journal

We believe food is better when you know a little about where it comes from and how it was raised.

At Backyard Farm, our focus is pasture-raised chickens offered as whole processed birds. This journal gives us a place to explain the choices we make on the farm, answer common questions, and share occasional updates.

What you'll find here

We plan to write about how we raise our chickens, the role of outdoor pens and daily care, what to expect when asking about a whole chicken, and announcements when we have product news to share.

Interested in a chicken?

Our website does not take online orders. If you'd like to know about an upcoming batch, current pricing, or pickup details, email the farm. We'll respond personally with the latest information.
